# EPISHIELD — Detect Early

## Director's treatment

- **Promise:** Show how separate environmental and community-health signals can become a clearer, earlier response workflow.
- **Audience:** Public-health and environmental-health teams, community partners, and people evaluating the EPISHIELD concept.
- **Takeaway:** Detect Early. Respond Faster. Protect Communities.
- **Format and runtime:** 16:9 landscape, 32 seconds.
- **Identity:** The project's ocean-blue and aqua palette, with Space Grotesk display type, DM Sans supporting type, and restrained JetBrains Mono labels. Use the name EPISHIELD as a typographic wordmark; no official logo asset is supplied.
- **Hero:** Existing project photography of a community response worker, water/environment monitoring, public-health signals, and AI analysis, connected by one recurring aqua ring.
- **Motion:** Photo-to-macro camera pushes; drawn signal arcs and map paths; compound ring, split-panel, and shape-match transitions.
- **Arc:** A human-scale observation becomes a labeled sample signal, then a conceptual map, a coordinated workflow, and a calm product lockup.
- **Payoff:** The ending resolves into a clear wordmark and the exact project tagline; its aqua ring then matches the opening image's ring for a seamless loop.
- **Scope:** This is an illustrative film for a static prototype. Every displayed value and map is sample artwork. It must not imply a live service, a real outbreak, a real location, clinical advice, or measured response outcomes.
- **Audio:** One original 32-second instrumental bed; no voiceover or sound effects. Beat alignment remains unverified until the generated track exists.

## Pre-code review

- **Distinct compositions:** The five shots alternate full-bleed human photo, environmental macro plus score, original map graphic, documentary photo/process montage, and a restrained typographic lockup. Adjacent shots differ in subject scale, graphic medium, framing, and primary action.
- **Beat density:** The opening changes at least every 0.5 seconds; every later shot has a visible action or text change at intervals under one second.
- **Safe and readable type:** All essential copy is within the 6% canvas inset. Hero word and score use large vmin sizing; explanatory labels stay at least 2.2vmin except for the persistent 1.8–2vmin footer.
- **No dead tails:** Each shot begins its outgoing handoff in the final 20% of its duration. The finale's loop transfer starts at 00:30.85 and continues through the final frame.
- **Disclosure:** Sample/prototype language remains on screen throughout. The score is marked illustrative; the map is marked non-live; the workflow is marked conceptual with no live service.
- **No unsupported claims:** No live detection, real location, outbreak, real notification, measured effectiveness, or clinical recommendation is shown or stated.
- **Audio plan:** One instrumental music bed only. No voiceover, dialogue, or sound effects are scripted.
